01 About

I'm 0xkagecrypt — a security researcher focused on Solana and the broader Rust-based chain ecosystem. My background is in software engineering, and these days I spend most of my time reading Anchor program source, breaking account validation logic, and chasing the edge cases that never show up in the happy path.

Solana pulled me in because the attack surface is different — there's no reentrancy guard to lean on, account ownership and PDA derivation are the whole game, and the tooling is still catching up to how fast the ecosystem is growing. Lately I've been pushing further into zero-knowledge proof systems, since a lot of the next wave of chain security is going to live there.
